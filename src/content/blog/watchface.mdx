##### What is Watchface?

Watchface was the first somewhat professional project that I worked on at UCI. Watchface is a smartwatch customization interface designed to help users create layouts for their devices. My role was to continue development after the original developer graduated. The project became a pivotal learning experience for me. You can visit the site here and get an idea of how it works <ProjectLink text="here" link="https://watchface-website.vercel.app/" />.

##### Challenges

Working on this project proved to be quite challenging.

1. I was first unfamiliar with Vue.js and had lacking experience in web development.
2. There was a lack of documentation and comments in the code.
3. I did not work on other people's code often, I usually worked as a solo developer.
4. The original developer was also hard to reach.

##### Overcoming Challenges

Initially, I gave myself too much pressure and stress to understand the entire codebase quickly. Although I put in the time and effort, I delayed my progress due to rushing the process. I stared at the code and clicked through random files, hoping something would click.

After several days of struggling, I tried different approaches. The best approach for me was to experiemnt with changing the files bit by bit and observing the changes.

Overtime, I managed to get a grasp the basics of the project.

##### Lessons Learned

I came into this project with unrealistic expectations. I thought that I had to understand the entire codebase. I was not patient and rushed to understand the code.

Through Watchface, I learned that I need to apply patience with my work.
