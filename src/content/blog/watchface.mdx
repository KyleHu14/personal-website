##### What is Watchface?

Watchface was a project that I worked on when I was a student at UCI. The website was for a project that aimed to research the best practices for smartwatch customization interfaces. The website served as the smartwatch customization interface where users could customize and create a potential smartwatch layout that they could download and use on their smartwatch. You can visit the site here and get an idea of how it works <ProjectLink text="here" link="https://watchface-website.vercel.app/" />.

My role in this project was to continue development of the website as the student who initially made the website had just graduated.
